Tested multiple font combinations. Learned that serif fonts carry the "old book" vibe better than anything else.

> "Cinzel plus Lora equals fantasy storybook. Hierarchy matters more than variety."

Cinzel Decorative for the site title, Cinzel for headings, Lora for body, Cormorant for nav, Mystery Quest for accents. Five fonts sounds like a lot, but each has one job. The hierarchy does the heavy lifting — not font variety for its own sake.
