# Image Asset Briefs

Assets that genuinely need raster image generation (painterly quality beyond honest flat SVG). The flat SVG versions already ship in `public/assets/mossy-ui/stickers/`; these briefs describe optional upgraded art for later.

MossyRealm palette for generated art: deep moss `#1e3028`, mid moss `#355a45`, amber `#e8a54b`, glow amber `#ffc857`, cream `#f5f0e1`, heading cream `#ffeaa7`, link green `#90ee90`, frog green `#7fae6a`, outline `#2d4a2f`, paper `#e8dcc0`, ink `#2b2a1e`, accent orange `#ff8c42`.

Style for ALL of the below: late-1990s clip-art warmth, slight film grain, flat-ish shading, visible 2px outline in `#2d4a2f`. No photorealism, no gradients beyond soft two-tone shading.

---

## 1. Frog at a desk (hero version)

- Subject: the MossyRealm frog sitting at a small wooden desk, one hand on a chunky beige keyboard, CRT glow on its face
- Composition: 3/4 view, frog right of center, desk across the bottom third
- Aspect: 4:3
- Background: transparent
- Palette: frog green, deep moss, amber (lamp), CRT green glow `#b7ff71`
- Intended page: /cabin landing (welcome panel)
- Output: 480×360 px PNG
- Keep empty: top-left ~40% for optional whisper-text overlay
